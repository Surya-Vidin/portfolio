If you can’t track it, you can’t scale it

Almost every business runs ads. Very few know which ad, keyword or post actually brought the customer. Tracking is the difference between spending and investing.

Without tracking

  • You spend ₹50,000 and get 40 calls, but can’t say from where.
  • Good ads and bad ads get the same budget.
  • Meta and Google learn from clicks, not from buyers.
  • Scaling feels like gambling.

With tracking

  • Ad A brought 22 leads at ₹380 each. Ad B brought 3 at ₹2,100. You pause B and double A.
  • Every lead carries its source, campaign and keyword.
  • The platforms optimize for real enquiries.
  • Scaling is a decision, not a guess. (Illustrative example)
